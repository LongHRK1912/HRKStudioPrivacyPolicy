What is AuraSleep?

AuraSleep is a next-generation smart sleep therapy application for Android designed to actively enhance sleep quality through real-time adaptive bio-acoustic soundscapes. Unlike static sound generators, AuraSleep functions as an intelligent closed-loop system that listens, analyzes, and dynamically tailors bio-acoustic audio (Binaural Beats, Pink/Brown Noise, Active Masking) based on your real-time physiological states and nocturnal sleep stages.

Core Features

  • Adaptive Bio-Acoustic Soundscapes: Real-time synthesized binaural beats, pink/brown noise, and atmospheric nature therapy engineered for brainwave entrainment (Delta & Theta frequency ranges).
  • Closed-Loop AI Sleep Staging: Synchronizes with sleep monitoring algorithms (Asleep API & edge TFLite models) to detect sleep stages (Wake, Light, REM, Deep) and seamlessly fade, alter, or quiet audio.
  • On-Device Snore & Noise Detection: Edge AI models continuously analyze nocturnal audio patterns locally to apply active noise masking when ambient disturbances occur.
  • CBT-I Cognitive Sleep Coaching: Evidence-based Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia tools, custom wind-down routines, sleep journals, and sleep efficiency tracking.
  • Smart Stage-Aware Bio-Alarm: Gently wakes you at the optimal lighter sleep phase to reduce morning sleep inertia and maximize morning energy.
  • Wear OS & Health Connect Sync: Seamless integration with Wear OS smartwatches and Google Health Connect to incorporate heart rate variability (HRV) and nocturnal body metrics into sleep insights.

Technological Innovation

AuraSleep features a high-performance native C++ Audio Engine built with AAudio/Oboe and AndroidX Media3/ExoPlayer, delivering sub-millisecond audio synthesis and spatial audio mixing. By leveraging lightweight TensorFlow Lite models directly on device, the app performs privacy-first audio classification for snore detection without streaming continuous raw audio recordings off your smartphone.

What is iOS Style Launcher?

iOS Style Launcher is a premium, high-performance launcher for Android that brings the sleek, elegant, and intuitive iOS aesthetic to your device. Designed with fluid animations, custom widgets, a fully functional App Library, and extensive icon customization options, it transforms your Android interface into a premium iOS-like experience. Built with performance and efficiency in mind, it provides a smooth, lag-free user experience without draining your battery.

Core Features

  • iOS Aesthetic & Smoothness: Enjoy fluid screen transitions, physics-based scroll animations, and a refined layout inspired by the latest iOS design.
  • App Library: Automatically categorize your apps into smart folders like Social, Games, Productivity, and search them instantly with a smart search bar.
  • Custom Widgets: Add premium iOS-style widgets for weather, calendar, battery, clocks, and usage stats directly to your home screens.
  • Icon Customization: Change individual app icons, customize icon sizes, rename apps, or hide sensitive applications to maintain a clean layout.
  • Dynamic Blur & Liquid Glass: Features beautiful real-time Gaussian blur and liquid glass visual effects across search bars and widgets.
  • Flexible Grids: Choose your preferred layout grids (5x4, 6x4, etc.) to perfectly fit your device's screen size and density.
  • Smart Notification Badges: Stay updated with elegant notification counts showing directly on application icons.
  • System Integration: Easy set-up as your default launcher, with optional shortcut links for lock screens, control centers, and assistive touch configurations.

Technological Innovation

iOS Style Launcher utilizes a custom high-performance rendering engine that optimizes screen drawing and animates layout components at 60 FPS (or higher on supported screens). By leveraging advanced Android WindowManager and custom RecyclerView configurations, the app ensures instant search responses and ultra-low latency transitions even on entry-level Android devices.

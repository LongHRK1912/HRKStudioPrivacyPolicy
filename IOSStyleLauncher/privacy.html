Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2026

1. Summary

iOS Style Launcher respects your privacy. We do not sell or share your personal information. Most configuration data remains stored locally on your device, and we only collect data necessary to provide and improve our launcher functions.

2. What Data We Collect

iOS Style Launcher collects and processes the following information to facilitate your home screen organization:

  • Installed Application List: To display and launch apps on your device, our launcher retrieves the list of installed applications. This data is processed locally on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.
  • Home Screen & Layout Preferences: Custom folder structures, custom icon names, hidden apps, and widget configurations. This information is stored securely in a local database on your device.
  • Notification Data: If you grant the Notification Listener permission, the app processes notification counts to show badges on icons. This data is read in real-time and remains strictly on-device.
  • Usage Analytics & Crash Logs: Anonymous crash logs and diagnostic info (via Firebase Crashlytics) to help us find and resolve app crashes and improve stability.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Display and organize your applications in the home layout and App Library.
  • Personalize the home screen according to your custom preferences, widget layouts, and hidden applications.
  • Render notification badges on app icons.
  • Detect and resolve application crashes, freezes, or performance bottlenecks.

4. On-Device Processing & Security

Local Storage: All app arrangements, hidden apps, custom app names, and widget configurations are saved on your device inside a secure Room database.

No Cloud Upload: None of your application usage statistics, search queries, or notification contents are uploaded to any server. Your launcher experience is fully private and self-contained.

5. Third-Party Services

iOS Style Launcher uses Google Firebase for the following services:

  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics

These services operate under Google's standard privacy policies.

6. Your Rights

  • You can clear your customized layouts and restore default launcher configurations at any time in the app settings.
  • You can revoke permissions (like Notification Access) in your system settings at any time.

7. Data Retention

Since your launcher settings are stored locally on your device, they are retained as long as the app is installed. Deleting or clearing the app storage will permanently erase all custom settings.
